Privacy Policy

Newport Beach Firefighters Association, keepingnewportsafe.com

This page explains what we collect when you submit a comment on this site, what we do with it, and how to reach us to change or remove it.

What we collect

When you submit the comment form we collect your first name, last name, street address, email address, your comments, and, if you choose to provide it, your cell phone number. Street address is collected so the City Council can identify comments from Newport Beach residents.

How we use it

  • To share your comment, name, and street address with the Newport Beach City Council in connection with the Fire Station 3 relocation.
  • To contact you by email about Fire Station 3 and related public safety issues.
  • To contact you by text message, only if you provided a cell phone number and checked the consent box.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Comments submitted to the City Council

Comments forwarded to the City Council may become part of the public record under the California Public Records Act and the Ralph M. Brown Act, and may be published in council agenda materials. Please do not include information in your comment that you would not want to appear publicly.

Text messages

Text messages are sent only to people who provided a cell phone number and affirmatively checked the consent box. Consent is not a condition of submitting a comment or of any service. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe, or HELP for assistance. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Storage

Form submissions are stored in the form service used by this website and are accessible only to authorized representatives of the Newport Beach Firefighters Association. We retain submissions for as long as the Fire Station 3 matter remains before the City, and then delete them.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete your information at any time by emailing info@keepingnewportsafe.com. You may unsubscribe from email using the link in any message, and from text messages by replying STOP.

Contact

Questions or concerns about this policy, or about the information we hold about you, may be sent to info@keepingnewportsafe.com.
